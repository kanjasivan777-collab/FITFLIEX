About FITFLIX

FITFLIX is a fitness calculator website designed to make everyday fitness calculations simple and easy to understand.

What We Provide

Our website provides useful calculators and information related to BMI, daily calorie requirements, water intake, running, jogging and food calories.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide simple and easy-to-use fitness tools that can help users understand basic fitness calculations.

Important Information

The results provided by FITFLIX calculators are estimates for general informational purposes. They should not be considered medical advice or a diagnosis.
