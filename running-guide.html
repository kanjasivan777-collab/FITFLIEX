🏃 Running Guide

Running is a form of aerobic exercise that can support cardiovascular fitness and overall physical activity.

🔥 What Can Affect Calories Burned?

  • Body weight
  • Running distance
  • Running speed and intensity
  • Terrain
  • Duration of exercise

📊 Using the FITFLIX Running Calculator

Enter your weight and running distance to get a general estimate of calories burned. Actual energy use can vary between individuals.

🏃 Beginner Tips

  • Start gradually and increase activity over time.
  • Wear comfortable and suitable footwear.
  • Warm up before harder exercise.
  • Stay hydrated.
  • Give your body enough time to recover.

⚠️ Important Note

Calorie-burn estimates are approximate and are not medical advice. Stop exercise and seek appropriate medical help if you experience concerning symptoms.
