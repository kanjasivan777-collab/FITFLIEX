Daily Calorie Guide

Calories are a measure of energy. The amount of energy a person needs each day varies depending on factors such as age, sex, body size and physical activity.

What Are Daily Calories?

Your daily energy requirement is the approximate amount of energy your body needs to support basic functions and physical activity.

What Can Affect Calorie Needs?

  • Age
  • Sex
  • Height and body weight
  • Daily physical activity
  • Exercise and lifestyle

Why Calorie Estimates Are Different

Two people of the same weight may have different calorie needs because their height, age, body composition and activity levels can differ.

Using the FITFLIX Calculator

You can use the FITFLIX Daily Calories calculator to get a general estimate based on the information you enter.

Important Note

Calorie calculations are estimates and are not medical advice. Individual nutritional needs can vary. If you have a medical condition or specific nutrition concerns, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian.
