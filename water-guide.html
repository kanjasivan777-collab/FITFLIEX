Water Intake Guide

Water is essential for many functions in the human body. Daily fluid needs can vary from person to person.

What Can Affect Water Needs?

  • Body size
  • Physical activity
  • Weather and temperature
  • How much you sweat
  • Food and overall fluid intake

Signs You May Need More Fluids

Thirst and darker-than-usual urine can be signs that you may need more fluids. However, these signs are not perfect measures for everyone.

Using the FITFLIX Water Calculator

Enter your weight into the FITFLIX Water Intake calculator to receive a general estimate. Treat the result as a starting point, not as a fixed medical requirement.

Important Note

Water requirements vary between individuals. People with certain medical conditions or specific fluid restrictions should follow advice from their healthcare professional.
