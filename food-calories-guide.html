🍎 Food Calories Guide

Calories are a measure of energy provided by food and drinks. The amount of calories in a food depends on its portion size, ingredients and method of preparation.

🥗 Why Are Calories Important?

Understanding calories can help you become more aware of your daily food intake. Your body's energy needs depend on factors such as age, body size, activity level and other individual factors.

🍚 Common Foods

  • Rice

  • Dates
  • Almonds

📏 Portion Size Matters

The same food can provide different amounts of calories depending on how much you eat. For example, a small portion and a large portion of the same food will not contain the same amount of energy.

🔎 Using the FITFLIX Food Calories Calculator

Select a food from the FITFLIX calculator and enter the amount you want to check. The calculator provides an approximate calorie value for reference.

💡 Healthy Eating Tips

  • Include a variety of foods in your diet.
  • Eat vegetables and fruits regularly.
  • Include protein-rich foods.
  • Drink enough water.
  • Pay attention to portion sizes.
  • Check nutrition labels when available.

⚠️ Important Note

Food calorie values are approximate and can vary by brand, ingredients, portion size and preparation method. This page provides general information and is not medical or dietary advice.
