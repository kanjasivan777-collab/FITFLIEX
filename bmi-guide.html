BMI Guide

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a commonly used calculation based on a person's weight and height. It can be used as a general screening measure for adults.

How BMI Is Calculated

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²

Common Adult BMI Categories

  • Below 18.5 — Underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9 — Healthy weight
  • 25.0 to 29.9 — Overweight
  • 30.0 or above — Obesity

Important Note

BMI is only a screening measure. It does not directly measure body fat and may not accurately represent health for every individual. Factors such as muscle mass, age and body composition can affect how BMI should be interpreted.

FITFLIX provides BMI calculations for general informational purposes and not as a medical diagnosis.
